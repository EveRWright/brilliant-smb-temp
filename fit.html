Fit

Not for the old way.

We do not work with “this is how we have always done it.” Invitation is a fit standard, not a velvet rope.

The screen, said once.

The work requires a principal who will actually move. Someone who will look at the week instead of the plaque, and who will change a decision, a handoff, or a habit if that is what the week needs. A team that has already decided the old way will stay does not need this practice.

A company that wants to sound transformed is a different request. So is a workshop so the deck can say it happened. We can refuse both. The refusal is the screen, not a slogan for the homepage.

For

A principal who will move

The work is allowed to change. The week can be named without a new values line to hide it. The team is expected to keep the change when we leave. Output is an acceptable test.

Not for

Sounding transformed

If the answer to the leak is the old way, this is the wrong room. A plaque, a review form, or a tool installed over an unmoved week will not be offered as the substitute.

What moving looks like.

Moving is not enthusiasm. It is a principal who will sit with the week, name the leak, and let a protected step be removed. It is a room that can change its mind. It is willingness to judge the work by whether Tuesday is different, not by whether the session felt aligned.

The first conversation is where that becomes clear. It is private. It is not a pitch for a room that has already decided.

If that is the work, the conversation is the door.

Nothing from it becomes a case study. Nothing from it becomes a number on this site.
