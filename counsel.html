What we will actually ask.

What the week is doing. Where a decision waits. Which handoff drops. What has already been tried, and whether it changed Tuesday or only the deck. Whether a tool is in the leak, or beside it. Whether the principal will move if the old way is the thing in the way.

We are not asking for a strategy document before the conversation. We are not asking for a performance-review form to stand in for the week. What matters most, in seven words: the human engine, not the statement.

If the answer is this is how we have always done it, we will say so. Invitation is a fit standard. The firm can refuse. That refusal happens in the room, not on a scorecard.
