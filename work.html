The work

A receipt is not a result.

The test is whether the work changed in a way someone can point at. The transfer is whether the team can keep that change when we leave.

The order is the method.

It does not have a name, and it will not get one. We look at the human system that produces the week. We tie what we change to productivity. We leave the organization able to run it. That order is the practice.

Training for its own sake is a receipt. So is organizational development whose success is that the company can say it was done. An engagement score is a receipt. A binder is a receipt. A retainer that exists so the knowledge does not leave is a receipt of a different kind. A consultant who keeps the knowledge has not finished.

01 — See

Name the leak

Plain language, with the people who run the work. Decisions, waits, handoffs, the work that exists to be reported. Not a survey in place of a look. Not the org chart in place of the week.

02 — Link

Tie it to output

Change the human element that causes the leak. A decision right. A handoff with an owner. A manager who removes a block instead of adding a form. The change has to be pointable in the week, not laminated in a deck.

03 — Leave

Keep it in the room

The team learns to see the next leak without us. Innovation follows an engine that can run on its own. It does not arrive as a closing workshop.

What the team has at the end.

A named leak. A change someone can point at on an ordinary Tuesday. The ability to run that change when we are not in the room. Managers who can tell the difference between a block and a form. A room that can change its mind without waiting for a new statement.

What they do not have, and should not need, is a score from the session, a reason to keep the retainer, or a poster that explains the work they already know how to do. Productivity is the link. Innovation is what becomes possible once that link holds.

The test

Tuesday moved

Someone can say what is different in the decision, the wait, or the handoff. If the only difference is that a workshop happened, the work did not.

The transfer

We are not required

The knowledge stays in the organization. The engagement is built to end. That is the difference between counsel and a dependency.

If the tool is part of the leak, it has its own room.

The practice does not become a lab. The specialist does not become the brand.
